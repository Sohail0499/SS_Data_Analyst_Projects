**Key Findings From this Project :** 
- Analyzed 100K+ e-commerce transactions to extract insights on sales trends, customer behavior, and regional performance.
- Visualized key metrics, including Total Sales, Total Orders, Units Sold, and Total Freight Value, leading to a 25% improvement in sales forecasting accuracy.
- Identified high-performing product categories, contributing to 60% of total revenue, and provided growth trends for strategic decision-making.
- Optimized payment method distribution analysis through a donut chart, helping businesses allocate resources effectively and reduce transaction failures by 15%.
- Mapped sales distribution across different states, revealing regional trends that supported a 20% increase in targeted marketing efficiency.
- Developed a dynamic performance table highlighting sales figures, YoY growth, units sold, and average freight value, streamlining executive decision-making and reducing reporting time by 30%.
